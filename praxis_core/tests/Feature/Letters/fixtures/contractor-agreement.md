# Project Contractor Agreement

**Reference:** issued by HQ › Documents (Agreement, `JBS/AGR/2026/001`)
**Date:** the date of issue

## Between

**JBS Praxis Limited**, a company registered in Nigeria under RC 9985884, whose registered office is at 21B Bode Ajakaiye Street, Lekki, Lagos ("**JBS Praxis**"),

and

**Jonathan Ndamzi Destiny**, of [address], Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria ("**the Contractor**"),

together "the Parties".

## 1. Engagement

1.1 JBS Praxis engages the Contractor as a **Full-Stack Engineer** on the project described in Schedule B ("**the Project**"), and the Contractor accepts.

1.2 The Contractor's responsibilities are set out in Schedule A. The role is deliberately broad. The Contractor:
- builds and owns features end to end;
- reviews and tests work built by others;
- contributes to JBS Praxis's AI integrations and automations;
- takes on other engineering work JBS Praxis reasonably assigns.

1.3 The Contractor works with the team that began the Project and reports to the engineering lead JBS Praxis names.

## 2. Term and review

2.1 This Agreement starts on **10 November 2026** and runs until the Project is completed, expected on **20 December 2026** ("**the Term**").

2.2 The Term is also the Contractor's review period. JBS Praxis assesses the Contractor's work on five things:
- the quality of the code;
- delivery against agreed estimates;
- the testing and review of others' work;
- communication;
- respect for the security and confidentiality rules in this Agreement.

2.3 If the Contractor's performance is satisfactory, the Contractor becomes a **permanent member of the JBS Praxis team** at the end of the Term. Permanent members are remunerated per project, on terms agreed in writing for each project. JBS Praxis confirms this in a letter. No further trial period applies.

2.4 If the performance is not satisfactory, this Agreement ends at the end of the Term, with no further obligation on either Party beyond those in clauses 5 to 9.

## 3. Remuneration

3.1 For the Project, JBS Praxis pays the Contractor a total of **₦350,000 (three hundred and fifty thousand naira)** in two instalments:
- **₦175,000** at the end of November 2026;
- **₦175,000** on completion of the Project and acceptance of the Contractor's work.

3.2 Each instalment is paid within **five (5) working days** of the Contractor's invoice, to the bank account the Contractor gives in writing.

3.3 The Contractor is responsible for their own taxes. JBS Praxis deducts withholding tax where Nigerian law requires it and gives the Contractor the receipt.

3.4 The remuneration covers all the work under this Agreement. Expenses are reimbursed only when JBS Praxis has approved them in writing beforehand.

## 4. Referral commission

4.1 The Contractor may introduce prospective clients to JBS Praxis, including organisations in Port Harcourt.

4.2 To qualify, an introduction must be recorded in JBS Praxis HQ (Leads) before the first meeting with the prospective client. Prospects already on record in HQ do not qualify.

4.3 For each qualifying client who signs a contract with JBS Praxis, the Contractor earns **5% of the value of that client's first contract, before VAT**. It is paid within **48 hours** of JBS Praxis receiving the client's first deposit. Schedule C gives an example.

4.4 The commission is due on payments JBS Praxis actually receives within 12 months of the introduction, including after this Agreement ends.

## 5. Way of working

5.1 The Contractor works remotely from Port Harcourt, using their own computer and internet connection. They are available for collaboration from 09:00 to 17:00 West Africa Time, Monday to Friday, including the team's daily check-in.

5.2 The Contractor uses only the accounts JBS Praxis provides for the work: the company email address, HQ, the JBS Praxis GitHub organisation, and any cloud accounts. Two-factor authentication must be on everywhere it is offered.

5.3 Code, credentials and client data never go into personal repositories, personal storage, public places or third-party AI tools that JBS Praxis has not approved. Secrets are never written into code.

## 6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Contractor keeps confidential everything they learn about JBS Praxis, its clients, the Project and their systems, data, code and business. This includes the identity of the client in Schedule B. The Contractor uses it only to do the work under this Agreement.

6.2 This obligation continues after this Agreement ends, without limit of time for client data and source code, and for five years for everything else.

6.3 The Contractor complies with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and with any data-handling rules the client imposes.

6.4 When this Agreement ends, or when JBS Praxis asks, the Contractor returns or deletes all confidential material and confirms it in writing.

## 7. Intellectual property

7.1 Everything the Contractor creates in the course of this Agreement belongs to JBS Praxis, or to its client where JBS Praxis's contract with that client says so, from the moment it is created. This covers code, designs, documents, models and inventions. The Contractor assigns all rights in it accordingly and waives any moral rights to the extent the law allows.

7.2 The Contractor may keep and use their general skills and knowledge. They may show their work in a portfolio only with JBS Praxis's written permission.

## 8. Non-solicitation

For twelve (12) months after this Agreement ends, the Contractor does not, without JBS Praxis's written consent:
- work directly for the client in Schedule B on the systems built in the Project;
- solicit any JBS Praxis client they dealt with;
- solicit any member of the JBS Praxis team.

## 9. Status

9.1 During the Term the Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Nothing in this Agreement creates an employment relationship, a partnership or an agency.

9.2 The Contractor does not commit JBS Praxis to any obligation without its written authority.

## 10. Ending this Agreement

10.1 Either Party may end this Agreement with **seven (7) days'** written notice.

10.2 JBS Praxis may end it at once for a serious breach, in particular of clauses 5, 6 or 7.

10.3 On termination, the Contractor is paid for accepted work in proportion to the Project completed. Clauses 4, 6, 7, 8 and 11 continue to apply.

## 11. General

11.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

11.2 The Parties first try to settle any dispute amicably, then by mediation in Lagos. Failing that, the courts of Lagos State have jurisdiction.

11.3 This Agreement, with its schedules, is the whole agreement between the Parties. It can be changed only in writing signed by both. It may be signed electronically.

## Schedule A: Role and responsibilities

**Role:** Full-Stack Engineer. The Contractor is expected to be versatile, to keep learning, and to take on larger projects over time with JBS Praxis's training and support.

- **Features:**
  - Build features assigned to the Contractor, front end to back end, and own them through to release.
  - Write the automated tests that go with them.
- **Quality:**
  - Review and test work built by other team members, and report what is found clearly.
  - Fix defects in the Contractor's own work promptly.
- **AI and automation:** contribute to JBS Praxis's AI integrations and workflow automations when assigned.
- **Ways of working:**
  - Follow the team's practices: version control, code review, release process and documentation.
  - Attend the daily check-in and keep tasks up to date in HQ.
- **Business development (optional):** introduce prospective clients under clause 4.

## Schedule B: The Project

- **The Project:** continuing the build of a sales-force platform for a pharmaceutical manufacturer, delivered with Tales Consulting. The client is named to the Contractor separately, under clause 6.
- **Period:** 10 November 2026 to 20 December 2026 (expected completion).
- **Remuneration:** ₦350,000 in total, paid as set out in clause 3.

| Instalment | Amount | When |
|---|---|---|
| First | ₦175,000 | End of November 2026, on invoice |
| Second | ₦175,000 | On completion and acceptance (expected 20 December 2026), on invoice |

## Schedule C: Referral commission example

A company in Port Harcourt, introduced by the Contractor and recorded in HQ on 1 December, signs a first contract worth ₦4,000,000 before VAT and pays its first deposit on 15 January.

- Commission: 5% × ₦4,000,000 = **₦200,000**.
- Paid within 48 hours of the deposit, by 17 January.
