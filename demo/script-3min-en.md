# Ask your company anything: the 3-minute script (English)

For `demo-3min-en.mp4`, 188.0 s (3:08), eight chapters. 268 words of narration, about 126 s of speech at
150 words a minute. The voice stays silent while a question is typed; times are where each line starts
(`voiceover.json` has the spoken forms and the checks). Every figure comes from
`ndali-demo/data/story-figures.json`.

## Narration

**Morning brief** (0:00)
07:45. Before the COO asks anything, Ndali Group reports: yesterday’s sales, cash in naira and CFA francs,
5 stock-outs, and who is in.

**People and approvals** (0:16)
At 08:15, a question about Apapa.
Seven people are late at Apapa: 5 clocked in after 07:10, and 2 aren’t in yet.
Three approvals are waiting: a leave request, an expense claim and a purchase order.
One click approves the leave.

**Projects and budgets** (0:41)
Then, the projects.
Four of the 8 projects are over budget, ₦38.2m in total.
The table goes to Excel: a formatted sheet with the question, the sources and the time.

**Sales and stock by depot** (1:04)
Next, sales and stock by depot.
Sales by depot, in naira and CFA francs, side by side.
Stock is lowest on noodles at Apapa: 3.1 days of cover.

**Cash and receivables** (1:27)
Cash this morning: ₦412.8m.
More than 60 days: ₦96.3m across 14 customers, and 28.4m FCFA in Douala.

**Security and the audit log** (1:50)
Now a different account: a sales associate in Ikeja.
The answer is a refusal. The role has no payroll permission, so nothing was read, and the request was logged.
The COO finds it at the top of the audit log, among the morning’s answers, exports and approvals.
And AI spend has a monthly cap, in naira.

**From question to action** (2:20)
Finally, from question to action.
A form, filled in: Wednesday at 10:00, Apapa depot, 4 attendees. The COO saves it.
In the mailbox, a supplier has moved a delivery.
The summary comes with the stock impact: 3.1 days of noodle cover at Apapa.
And a reply is drafted, asking for a part delivery of 800 cartons.

**End card** (2:56)
Straight answers, from your own data. Ask your company anything.
