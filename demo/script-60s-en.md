# Ask your company anything: the 60-second script (English)

Narrated version `demo-60s-en.mp4` and silent loop `hero-60s-loop.mp4`, 61.0 s. 80 words of narration, about
37 s of speech at 150 words a minute. The voice stays silent while a question is typed; times are where
each line starts (`voiceover.json` has the spoken forms and the checks). Every figure comes from
`ndali-demo/data/story-figures.json`.

## Narration

**1 · Morning brief** (0:00.3)
07:45 in Lagos. The company reports before the COO asks.

**2 · People and approvals** (0:10.9, after the question is sent)
Seven late at Apapa, 3 approvals waiting. One click approves the leave.

**3 · Projects and budgets** (0:20.2)
Four projects are over budget, ₦38.2m in total. The evidence goes to Excel, sources included.

**4 · Security by role** (0:29.1, before typing; 0:34.1, after the refusal)
A sales associate asks.
Refused by role, and logged.

**5 · From question to action** (0:41.7)
A form, filled in. Saved, and in the calendar.

**6 · Work done, not just answered** (0:46.2)
A supplier’s delay, summarised with the stock impact. The reply is drafted, ready to send.

**7 · End card** (0:54.4)
Straight answers, from your own data. Ask your company anything.
