# Projects: one page per project, with its milestones, their task boards and the backlog (runbook for PR 7)

Agreed with the owner on 1 November 2026, after MyCity App ERP was imported (`docs/OPPORTUNITIES.md` 12) and the owner asked where a sprint can be seen and edited, how weight, notes and dependencies are set, and how work is added mid-project. Before PR 7, HQ had an "All projects" list (`ActiveDeployments`), one sprint board per project (`SprintBoard`: task columns by status, "Assign to me", drag to move, timers), the QA hub, timers and sign-offs, and *Scrum master* (AI backlog draft). There was no project page, nothing could be edited after it was created, tasks had no due date or dependencies, and milestones (`project_phases`) had no dates.

Later the same day the owner folded sprints into milestones (decision 9): **a milestone is the unit of work**, with its own dates and its own task board. HQ never says "sprint" any more.

The model to follow is MyCity's My Workspace (`praxisjbs/mycity-app`, `docs/guides/workspace.md`): a task panel with a lead and the people sharing it, watchers, steps, comments, blockers, "waits on" links, due dates and history; a board per project.

> **Every chat working on this reads this whole file first.** Record changes to its contracts in section 9 in the same commit.

## 1. Owner decisions (1 Nov 2026, binding)

| # | Question | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where milestones and the backlog live | **One project page with tabs**: Overview, Milestones (kanban), Milestone board, Backlog, QA, Meetings and money. Everything opens in a **drawer** to see and edit. Today's sprint board becomes the milestone board. |
| 2 | Milestone kanban columns | **Not started → In progress → Client review → Accepted → Invoiced → Paid.** |
| 3 | Weight | **One weight, meaning the same everywhere**: a milestone's weight is its share of the contract (the SOW's %, e.g. M1 12%); tasks carry no weight of their own. Progress: a milestone's is its done tasks over its tasks; the project's is the sum of each milestone's weight × its progress. Story points stay in the database (timers read them) but are no longer shown or asked for. |
| 4 | People on a task | **A lead (picked from the team: a picker, not only "Assign to me") + people sharing it + watchers**, as in MyCity. Watchers are told when it moves to Done or Client review, or gets a comment. |
| 5 | Dependencies | **Warn only**: a task can wait on tasks, a milestone on milestones; the drawer and the card show "Waits on …" and a warning while the other isn't done, but nothing is ever stopped. Loops and self-links are refused. |
| 6 | Deadlines | **Due dates and overdue flags only**: a due date on tasks and milestones (a milestone's due date is its end), and a red *Overdue* badge when one passes unfinished. No reminders or late reasons for now. |

A milestone or task that changes the price goes into a **change report** (SOW clause 7.1): price, effect on the timeline, and the client's decision. Approved, it joins the plan and the contract total; declined, it is kept as declined (MyCity's Change Report 01, Mailbox, was declined on 1 Nov). |
| 8 | Who edits | **The project lead and managers edit everything** (milestones, any task, change reports). **Team members move and update their own tasks** (status, steps, comments, due date proposals visible to the lead). |
| 9 | Sprints and milestones (1 Nov, later) | **One thing, called "Milestone" everywhere.** The word "sprint" disappears from HQ: the task board is the **milestone board**, "Sprint boards" becomes "Milestone boards", there is no sprint drawer and no *New sprint*. A milestone carries its start date, due date (its end), status, goal, notes and acceptance; its board reads its tasks through `tasks.phase_id`. **Every task has a milestone**: there is no milestone-less backlog (ideas wait in a 0% milestone such as "Later"). A milestone lasts as long as the SOW says; Overdue once its due date passes and it isn't accepted. Weight is unchanged; there is no separate sprint weight. |

## 2. The project page (`/operations/projects/{id}`)

Opened from All projects, the milestone boards list, the opportunity's Project tab and the QA hub. Header: client, title, lead, dates (start, target end), contract total and currency, overall progress (decision 3), the next milestone due, and counts of overdue tasks and open QA tickets.

- **Overview**: milestones as a strip with weight, status, due date and progress (each opens its board); the milestones under way (in progress or client review, or running today by their dates), with dates, progress and goal; overdue items; recent activity (from the audit log); the project lead (managers change it).
- **Milestones**: the kanban of decision 2. A card shows code and title, weight %, due date (and Overdue), progress, "Waits on …" warnings, and the invoice badge; **opening a card shows its task board**, and its pencil opens the milestone drawer. Dragging between Not started, In progress, Client review and Accepted sets `project_phases.status` (Accepted asks for the acceptance date and who accepted, e.g. "Oyebode, CTO, 22 Oct"). **Invoiced** and **Paid** follow the milestone's invoice: dragging to Invoiced opens Finance's *Create invoice* prefilled (needs the finance permission; otherwise it says who can); Paid follows the invoice being marked paid in Finance. *New milestone* adds one (decision 7).
- **Milestone board**: one milestone's tasks by status (To do, In progress, Client review, Changes requested, Done): today's sprint board, kept as its component (`SprintBoard`) and now taking a milestone, with its Milestone menu (opening on the one running now) and *Milestone details* (the drawer). Timers, the 45-minute check and the client review window are unchanged.
- **Backlog**: every task of the project, grouped by milestone, filterable by lead, status and Overdue; *New task* under any milestone; a task opens the same drawer.
- **QA**: the project's QA tickets (link to the QA hub filtered to it).
- **Meetings and money**: links to the opportunity's Meetings tab (transcripts, handovers) and SOW for whoever can open the opportunity (its team included), the project's invoices and change reports.

## 3. Drawers (side panels; edit in place; Escape closes; focus returns)

- **Milestone**: code and title, description, weight % (the project's milestones should add up to 100%: show the total and warn when they don't), status, start date, end (due date), goal, accepted on/by, notes, waits on (other milestones), its tasks (with progress) and a link to its board, its invoice. Delete only when it has no tasks or invoice.
- **Task**: title, description, milestone (required), **lead** (a picker over staff on the project or with project rights, with "Make me the lead"), people sharing it, watchers (pickers too), status, due date (Overdue badge), estimated hours, steps (a checklist), waits on (other tasks of the project), comments (`task_comments`, with the existing attachment path), history (from the audit log), client-visible switch (`tasks.is_client_visible`). "Assign to me" stays as a shortcut at the top.
- **Change report**: number (CR-01…), title, description, price and currency, effect on the timeline (days), status (Draft, Sent, Approved, Declined), the client's decision (date, name, title), the milestone and tasks it adds (drafted inside it, joining the plan only when Approved), and on approval the contract total grows by its price.

### PR 5b (1 Nov 2026): the planner

The owner's seven decisions of 1 Nov 2026 (relayed by the lead chat), built in `App\Support\Projects\Planner` (rules), `App\Livewire\Hq\Operations\ScrumMaster` (the screen, `hq.ops.scrum`, still `/operations/scrum-master/{id}`) and `App\Listeners\Projects\FilePlanAi`. Strings in `lang/{en,fr}/planner.php` (a test checks both have the same keys); PR 5's `projects.planner` strings are gone. **No direct Gemini call is left**: everything goes through `Ai::queue`, in the background, within the budget.

- **Where it opens**: *Plan milestones* on the project page's header (new, for whoever may manage the project) and on All projects, as before.
- **Three modes** (`Planner::mode`): `draft_pending` (no milestones and an uncreated project draft on its opportunity: the planner says so and links to the opportunity's Project tab, PR 5's route); `milestone` (the project has milestones: plan one at a time); `whole` (no milestones and no project draft, e.g. no opportunity: the AI drafts milestones and tasks, saved through `PlanWriter` as before, two weeks each from today with weights shared out, refused once the project has milestones).
- **The scope box** is filled from the project's opportunity, in this order, and stays editable: the signed SOW (and its milestones), the client-approved proposal, the questionnaire's answers, the meetings' AI notes, then the approved change reports (`- CR-02 title: description`). Each part through `OpportunityContext::for($o, include: [part])`; all of it only for someone who may open the opportunity (`OpportunityAccess::canView`). **Someone who can't** (e.g. a project lead without opportunity rights) gets only what the project's screens show (`Planner::projectScope`, the lead's audit decision): the organisation's name and sector, the signed SOW's sections and milestones without its fees (sections about fees, prices, payment, invoices, costs, budget or VAT are left out, and so are the milestones' weights), and the change reports; never the proposal, its line items, the turnover or the budget. A short line under it says which sources were used ("signed statement of work SOW/…, the approved proposal, notes from 2 meetings, 1 approved change report"). A project without an opportunity starts empty (the lead pastes the scope); a legacy project with an Authorized or Converted proposal gets that proposal's text. At least 50 and at most 60,000 characters.
- **Per milestone** (purpose `plan_milestone`, Sonnet, effort medium, 16,000 tokens, streamed; about $0.05–0.15 a run, up to about $0.30 with a transcript): the AI reads the scope box as context and, as material, **the adopted plan** (every milestone with its code, weight, dates and status, its tasks numbered `T1…` with status, estimate, title and the start of the detail; the chosen milestone marked *TO PLAN*, its own tasks with their detail **in full, lines kept**). The scope, the plan and a transcript are fenced (`BEGIN SCOPE` … `END SCOPE`, `ADOPTED PLAN`, `TRANSCRIPT`; a marker line inside the text is dropped) and the task says text inside them is a record, never instructions. It returns new tasks for that milestone (title, detail = what done looks like, estimate in hours; **never a lead**), suggested edits to that milestone's own tasks (**only what it changes**: `""` keeps the title or detail, `0` the estimate; a new detail replaces the whole old one; and why), and notes on any task that looks duplicated or outside the SOW. It never removes a task: there is no such output. HQ files at most 30 new tasks, 40 edits and 30 notes; drops a new task whose title an existing task already has; stores an edit's fields only where they differ (null = unchanged) with **the task as the AI read it** (`plan_suggestions.base`: title, detail, estimate, milestone), one edit per task; and turns task numbers back into tasks with `ai_runs.input.task_ids` (kept on the run, never sent).
- **Transcripts on demand, one meeting only**: when the person may read the opportunity's transcripts (`OpportunityAccess::canReadTranscripts`), the first pass also gets a numbered list of the meetings that have a transcript (title and date, no text) and may answer `read_transcript: {meeting_number, why}`. HQ then queues a **second pass** with that one meeting's transcript (up to 120,000 characters) and the reason; the second pass can't ask again, and its answer is filed. The run's input carries its pass, and a run whose pass isn't the plan's is never filed, so a page polling while the second pass is queued can't file the first pass over it (the lead's audit). Without transcript rights, no list is offered and nothing is read. No transcript is ever sent up front. The screen says "It asked for the transcript of “…” and is planning again with it" while the second pass runs. If the AI service refuses the second pass (budget), the first pass's suggestions are filed, only if the plan still waits for them, and the plan goes back to pass 1.
- **Review** (the right-hand card): the whole adopted plan, every milestone collapsed to one line (code · title, its suggestion and note counts, dates, status, tasks done/total); tap to open, several open at once, *Expand all* / *Collapse all*. Suggestions sit inside their milestone, marked *New* or *Suggested edit* with a tick box; an edit shows each field it changes **side by side** (*Now* | *Suggested*; stacked at 375 px), and an edit whose task changed since the AI read it says so and can't be ticked. Notes are marked *Note* (nothing to tick). **Save the ticked suggestions (n)** writes only those: new ones through `Tasks::create` (no lead, no per-task bell), edits through `Tasks::update` with **only the fields the AI changed** (each logged, so they show in the task's history), and only if the task (row locked) still has the title, detail, estimate and milestone the AI read: otherwise the edit is **refused** (`status` refused) and named ("Not changed, because the task changed after the AI read it: “…”"); unticked ones are set aside. The message says what was done ("Added 1 task. Changed 2 tasks. The other suggestions were set aside."). Then one audit line `PLAN_SAVED` and **one bell per person** whose tasks changed ("The plan for “…” changed 3 of your tasks", in `staff_locale`, opening the backlog filtered to them). Nothing removes a task or adds a milestone.
- **Tables** (migration `2026_11_02_100000_create_milestone_plans`): `milestone_plans` (project, `phase_id` = the milestone planned or null for a whole plan, `scope`, `sources`, `status` drafting | ready | saved | failed, `ai_run_id`, `pass`, `transcript_meeting_id`, `transcript_reason`, `output` (the whole plan), `revision`, audit columns, soft deletes); `plan_suggestions` (plan, `phase_id`, `kind` new | edit | note, `task_id`, `title`, `detail`, `estimate_hours`, `why`, `position`, `base`, `status` pending | accepted | rejected | refused, `decided_by`, `decided_at`). ### VAT switch per company (owner, 1 Nov 2026)

- **The rule**: Finance adds VAT to an invoice only when the JBS Praxis company billing it charges VAT on the day the invoice is raised.
  - The billing company is the one chosen on the project's opportunity, else Nigeria's company.
  - Each company has its own switch, VAT number and start date in **Master data › Legal entities**. The rate is the company's country rate in Tax compliance, decided by `App\Support\Finance\Vat::for()`.
  - For a client in another country, the invoice is zero-rated (0%, an exported service).
  - **Every switch starts off**, so Finance's *Create invoice* adds no VAT today, and the deposit invoice raised on signing doesn't either.
  - Each invoice records `vat_applied`, `vat_rate` and its company: `billing_entity_id`, plus its region in `region_code`.
- **VAT paid on purchases (input VAT)**: not handled yet. It starts once a company is registered (owner, 1 Nov 2026).
- **Milestone pre-fills** are still before VAT (the rule above). Finance's *Create invoice* now says, under the form, whether VAT will be added and why ("No VAT: JBS Praxis Nigeria Ltd doesn’t charge VAT yet."), and so does the milestone drawer's line "Its invoice: …".
- **MyCity App ERP** (₦3.7M, no VAT, imported): its invoices are untouched. With Nigeria's switch off, its remaining pre-fills (M2 ₦288,000 … M11 ₦128,000, above) carry **no VAT**. Before this change, Finance would have added Nigeria's 7.5% on top (₦21,600 on M2). The migration was checked on a copy of the PR 7 dev database: MyCity's two invoices and their `updated_at` were the same after it. Once Nigeria's company is switched on, MyCity's milestone invoices raised from the start date get VAT, as any other invoice does. The owner confirmed on 1 Nov 2026 that invoices take the VAT of their date.

### Less deposit, every milestone (owner, 1 Nov 2026)

- **The rule**: when a project's signed SOW raised a deposit invoice, each of the SOW's milestones is invoiced at **its amount × (1 − the deposit's share)**, so the deposit and the milestone invoices add up to exactly the contract. `project_phases.amount` stays the milestone's full share of the contract; only what is due changes. `App\Support\Projects\Deposit`.
- **The deposit's share** = the deposit invoice's amount before VAT ÷ the contract before VAT as signed (the SOW's approved proposal's subtotal; for an imported SOW, the project's total less the change reports approved since). A cancelled deposit invoice counts as none. A milestone a **change report** added isn't reduced (the deposit never covered it). A project without a SOW deposit is unchanged. When the reduced milestones' amounts make the whole contract, the last one takes the rounding, so the total is exact to the cent.
- **Where it shows**: Finance's *Create invoice*, opened from a milestone dragged to Invoiced, is pre-filled with the amount due and the line "M1 …, on written acceptance, less the 40% deposit"; the milestone drawer says "Its invoice: M1 · ₦444,000 less 13.51% deposit = ₦384,000, before VAT." Amounts are before VAT: Finance adds VAT per invoice. Invoices already issued never change.
- **Before VAT**: a project made from a PR 5 SOW keeps its contract total (`projects.total_budget`) and its milestones' amounts **before VAT** (the approved proposal's subtotal), because Finance adds VAT to whatever it pre-fills. Legacy projects made by `ProposalGateway` before the VAT switch (1 Nov 2026) keep their total VAT included, so their pre-fill carries VAT that Finance would tax again. Since that change, the gateway also stores the subtotal and the milestone amounts before VAT.
- **MyCity App ERP** (imported: deposit INV/…/2026-001 ₦500,000 before VAT on a ₦3,700,000 contract, so 13.51%): M1 was invoiced at ₦384,000 (₦444,000 less 13.51%) before this rule, so live already follows it. The pre-fills of the milestones not yet invoiced become: M2 ₦333,000 → ₦288,000; M3 ₦185,000 → ₦160,000; M4 ₦481,000 → ₦416,000; M5 ₦407,000 → ₦352,000; M6 ₦407,000 → ₦352,000; M7 ₦370,000 → ₦320,000; M8 ₦296,000 → ₦256,000; M9 ₦259,000 → ₦224,000; M10 ₦370,000 → ₦320,000; M11 ₦148,000 → ₦128,000 (M0 is 0%). Deposit ₦500,000 + M1–M11 due ₦3,200,000 = ₦3,700,000.
