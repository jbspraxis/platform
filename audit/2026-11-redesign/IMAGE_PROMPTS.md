## Shared style block (paste at the end of every prompt)

```
Style: documentary architectural photography, shot on a full-frame camera with a 35mm lens, natural light only, photorealistic, not CGI, not an illustration. Composition favours order: strong horizontal lines, rows, symmetry, generous negative space in the upper third for a headline. Colour grade: cool and slightly desaturated (about -15% saturation), shadows leaning towards deep petrol blue (#044B6A), clean neutral highlights, never tinted fully teal, no orange-and-teal look. Sharp detail, fine natural grain, realistic dust and wear. No people facing the camera, no identifiable faces; any people are small, distant, in motion or seen from behind. No logos, no signage, no readable text, no licence plates, no brand colours on vehicles or packaging. No glow, no holograms, no digital overlays, no lens flare, no drone-ad gloss. Landscape 3:2, high resolution.
```

## 1. Pharmaceuticals: quality-control corridor

```
A long, spotless quality-control laboratory corridor in a modern pharmaceutical manufacturing plant in West Africa. Glass walls on both sides reveal stainless-steel instruments, sealed sample cabinets and neatly labelled racks (labels unreadable). Polished pale epoxy floor reflecting cool overhead light; one distant technician in a white coverall and hairnet walking away from the camera, small in the frame. Symmetrical one-point perspective, calm and exact, early morning light from a high window at the far end.
```

## 2. Consumer goods: distribution hub at dusk

```
A large consumer-goods distribution warehouse on the edge of Douala, Cameroon, at dusk. The loading dock doors are open in a long even row, warm interior light spilling onto a wet concrete yard. Unbranded white box trucks reversed into the bays at identical angles, shrink-wrapped pallets of crates and returnable bottle cases stacked in straight lines. Tropical evening sky in deep blue with a thin band of last light on the horizon. Wide, low viewpoint, strong horizontal lines.
```

## 3. Oil and gas: terminal at first light

```
An onshore oil and gas terminal in the Niger Delta, Nigeria, at first light. Rows of large white storage tanks and a long straight pipe rack running to the horizon, a thin morning mist over flat mangrove wetland beyond. Two distant workers in coveralls and hard hats walking along a raised walkway, seen from behind, tiny in the frame. Pale gold sunrise low on the horizon, the rest of the sky clear and cool. Scale and discipline, very ordered, wide angle from slightly above.
```

## 4. Logistics: container port in the early morning

```
A container terminal at a West African seaport near Lagos, Nigeria, in the early morning. Containers in long, exact rows, plain weathered colours with no company names, ship-to-shore gantry cranes standing in a line along the quay, a large unbranded container ship berthed. Soft haze over the water, wet asphalt lanes between the stacks with one straddle carrier in motion. Elevated viewpoint looking along the rows so the lines lead to the horizon.
```

## 5. Lagos from above

```
An aerial view of Lagos, Nigeria, in the late afternoon: the dense grid of roofs, roads and bridges across the lagoon, with the Lekki and Victoria Island towers in the distance. Traffic reduced to small streaks of light on the main expressway, the lagoon water calm and silver. Shot from high altitude looking slightly down, so the city reads as a pattern and a system, not as landmarks. Long, soft shadows, haze in the distance.
```

## 6. Douala and the Wouri river from above

```
An aerial view of Douala, Cameroon, at dusk, where the city meets the wide Wouri river: the long road bridge crossing the water, port cranes and warehouses along the riverbank, the city grid spreading inland under a deep blue sky. First streetlights coming on in neat lines, the river reflecting the last light. High viewpoint, calm and ordered, the river and bridge forming strong horizontal lines across the frame.
```
