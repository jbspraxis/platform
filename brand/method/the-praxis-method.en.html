Formerly the Praxis Framework.

Every organisation has two versions of itself. One is on paper: the policies, the organisation chart, the budget, the process map. The other is how the work really gets done.

In the second version, numbers are rebuilt by hand at month end, approvals wait for one person, and decisions are made without the facts. Email sits in one place, chat in another, accounts in a third, approvals somewhere in between. Each tool answers for itself; none answers for the organisation. The distance between the two versions is where money leaks, people tire and boards lose confidence.

Order, made visible

Praxis means theory put into practice. JBS Praxis does two things. First we make the order: we design how money, people, operations and risk should flow. Then we make it visible: we build the system that shows, every day, whether they do. Consulting makes the order; engineering makes it visible.

One method for every engagement

The Praxis Method is how we do both. It has four stages, See, Design, Build and Hold, and it works on one area of your organisation at a time. Each stage ends with something you can read, check or use, and the same team stays with you from the first meeting to handover.

The result is an organisation that can answer for itself: a straight answer to every question about your money, people, operations and risk, from a system your own team can run.

Stage 2 of 4

DesignWe agree how it should run before anything is built.

The way of working first, the system second.

That is the order of every engagement.

We design how the area should run: the process, the controls, who approves what, and the few numbers that matter.

The design is about how your organisation works, not about software. It sets out the work so that the people who do it, and the people who answer for it, can read it, question it and sign it off.

What the design covers

  • The process. Each step in order, who does it, and what it needs.
  • The controls. Checks built into the work rather than added after, including separation of duties: the person who prepares a payment cannot approve it.
  • The roles. Who can view, create, edit, approve or export, so people see what their job needs and nothing more.
  • The numbers that matter. The few figures leaders need, and where each one comes from.
  • The terrain. IFRS and OHADA, naira and CFA francs, English and French, and the way your organisation really works.

After Design

You receive

A design you can read and sign off.

Who decides

You do. You sign off the design, and the build follows it.

Stage 4 of 4

HoldWe stay until the system holds, then hand over the keys.

We run on it ourselves

Our own invoicing, payroll, projects and approvals run on the same system we build for clients.

A system is finished when your own team runs it. So we stay: we train your people, watch the first cycles, fix what the real world reveals, then hand over the keys.

What holding means

  • Training. Your people learn to run the system on their own work, in English or French.
  • The first cycles. We watch the system through its first real cycles, such as a month end or a payroll run, and fix what they reveal.
  • Handover. The keys pass to your team, with the code and the data that are yours.

How the system keeps running

After handover, your team runs the system. Every action stays permissioned by role and recorded in an audit log, so it is always clear who changed what, and when. If you want us to stay on, ongoing support and maintenance can continue after handover.

You receive

Security and data

Your data stays yours, and every sensitive action leaves a record.

The systems we build protect what matters by design: who can see it, who can change it, and a record of everything.

Roles and permissions

Every screen and action is allowed by role (view, create, edit, approve or export), so people see what their job needs and nothing more.

A record of everything

Sensitive actions are written to an audit log: who did what, when and from where.

Separation of duties

The person who prepares a payment or a payroll run cannot approve it. Controls are built into the work, not added after.

Private by default

Documents, CVs, receipts and payment proofs are stored privately and only opened through signed, permissioned links.

Aligned with ISO 27001

Our processes follow ISO 27001 practices. We do not claim a certification we do not hold.

Your rights

We work under Nigeria’s Data Protection Act (NDPA) and Cameroon’s data protection law. You can ask what we hold about you, correct it or have it deleted.

Questions about your data? Write to info@jbspraxis.com. The full page is at jbspraxis.com/security.
